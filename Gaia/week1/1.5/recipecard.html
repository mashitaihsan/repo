Buttermilk Chicken

Creamy and delicious buttermilk chicken with crispy chicken coated in a rich, buttery sauce. Perfect for lunch or dinner!

Ingredients:

  • 500g chicken breast, cut into pieces
  • 1/2 cup buttermilk
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup evaporated milk
  • 5 curry leaves
  • 2 red chilies, sliced

Instructions:

  1. Marinate the chicken with buttermilk, salt, and black pepper for 30 minutes.
  2. Coat the marinated chicken pieces with flour and garlic powder.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and fry the chicken until golden brown and crispy.
  4. Remove the chicken and set it aside.
  5. In the same pan, melt the butter and sauté the garlic until fragrant.
  6. Add curry leaves and sliced red chilies.
  7. Pour in the evaporated milk and stir well.
  8. Simmer the sauce until it becomes slightly thick and creamy.
  9. Add the crispy chicken back into the pan and coat it with the sauce.
  10. Serve hot with rice and enjoy!
